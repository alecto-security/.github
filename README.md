# We Move First.

Most companies want to be liked.

We don't.

We exist to protect innocent people, move faster than the systems that fail them, and act when everyone else is still debating what to do.

We are building a company around one belief:

**Good people should not have to wait for bad things to happen before someone acts.**

## Who We Are

We are not the polished hero.

We are the thing that shows up when the polished heroes are too slow.

We move aggressively. We question assumptions. We make decisions quickly. We take responsibility for the consequences of those decisions.

People may not always like how we operate.

That is not our metric.

**Did we protect the person who needed protecting?
Did we stop the threat before it became damage?
Did we act when others hesitated?**

That is what matters.

## Why We Exist

Too much harm happens in the gap between **knowing something is wrong** and **someone finally doing something about it**.

Organizations hesitate.

Committees meet.

Warnings get ignored.

Responsibility gets passed around.

Meanwhile, innocent people pay the price.

We intend to close that gap.

Our goal is simple:

**See danger earlier. Move faster. Protect people.**

## Speed Is a Moral Advantage

Speed is not a feature for us.

It is part of the mission.

When someone is in danger, being right eventually is not enough.

The company that understands the situation in ten minutes can outperform the company that takes ten days.

The company that acts today can prevent what everyone else explains tomorrow.

We intend to become relentlessly fast.

Not reckless.

Not careless.

**Decisive.**

## We Are Not Neutral About Harm

We are biased toward the innocent.

We are biased toward the person being exploited, threatened, manipulated, attacked, or ignored.

We are biased toward stopping preventable harm.

We are not interested in pretending every situation has two equally defensible sides when the facts clearly say otherwise.

We follow evidence.

We make the call.

We act.

## We Do Not Optimize for Approval

There will always be people who dislike companies that move quickly, challenge established systems, or refuse to behave like everyone else.

Fine.

We are not building this company to win popularity contests.

We will listen to criticism when it exposes something true.

We will change when the evidence proves us wrong.

But we will not become slower, weaker, or less effective simply because effectiveness makes someone uncomfortable.

**Reputation follows the mission. The mission does not follow reputation.**

## Our Code

1. **Protect innocent people.**
2. **Move before bureaucracy catches up.**
3. **Follow evidence, not pressure.**
4. **Never confuse hesitation with responsibility.**
5. **Take ownership when we make a decision.**
6. **Admit when we are wrong and correct course immediately.**
7. **Do not become the thing we are fighting.**
8. **Never sacrifice innocent people for optics.**
9. **Stay dangerous to people who intend to cause harm.**
10. **Stay trustworthy to everyone else.**

## What We Want People to Feel

At first:

**Who are these people?**

Then:

**They actually did something.**

Eventually:

**If something goes wrong, I want them there.**

We don't need to look harmless.

We need innocent people to know that when something matters, **we show up**.

## The Standard

We want to build the fastest, most relentless company in our category.

A company predators hate dealing with.

A company bureaucracies cannot outpace.

A company innocent people are glad exists.

We are not here to look heroic.

**We are here to make sure the right people are protected.**
